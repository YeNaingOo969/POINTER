What is POINTER?

POINTER is a city guide for Yangon. Find the places worth visiting, see what shops are offering today and read what people really think — all on one map, and free for everyone.

  • 01 Find places

    Pagodas, tea shops, hotels, markets, hospitals and spas — sorted into seven simple groups.

  • 02 See deals

    Shops post their own discounts. Tap for directions and get a ticket to show at the counter.

  • 03 Read reviews

    One honest review per person for each place. Owners can reply, but they can never delete one.

Why is it free?

The more people use it, the better it gets.

POINTER is only useful when lots of people use it, and shops pay because those people are here. If users paid, fewer would come — and the app would be worth less to every shop.
